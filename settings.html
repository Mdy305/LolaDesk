Boulevard — Lola books straight into it

Lola checks your real Boulevard availability, books the appointment in Boulevard, and reads it back to verify before she tells the client it’s booked. It also appears in LolaDesk and on the client’s card.

1 · Business ID: Boulevard → Settings → Calendar Integrations — it’s the long ID inside your private calendar link.
2 · API key: create an app at developers.joinblvd.com, then install it in Boulevard → Settings → Apps & Integrations.
Tip: if Boulevard requires a card to book online, Lola texts the client your booking link for that last step — turn the card requirement off in Boulevard for fully hands-free booking.

Any booking system — by calendar link

Keep the system you use. In Vagaro, Boulevard, Fresha, Mindbody, GlossGenius, Google or Apple, copy the private “subscribe” / iCal link (one for the salon, or one per stylist) and paste it here. Lola sees those appointments within minutes and never books over them.

Boulevard (or any system) — two-way through Zapier

No partner approval needed. Your booking system tells Lola about every appointment within seconds, and Lola’s bookings block that time in your system.
In Zapier: trigger Boulevard → New Appointment, action Webhooks by Zapier → POST to this link. Data: event = new, id, start, end, staff, service, client. Copy the Zap twice for Appointment Rescheduled (event = rescheduled) and Appointment Cancelled (event = cancelled).
