Quick answers

How do I get started?

Go to Get started, create your workspace, and Lola gets her own local number. Add your website, Google Maps and Instagram links and she learns your services, prices, team and hours. Forward your salon line to Lola or publish her number.

How much does it cost?

Starter $99/month, Pro $399/month, Med-Spa $599/month, each with a 14-day free trial and no card to start. Details at loladesk.com/pricing. Cancel anytime in Settings → Subscription.

My clients aren’t getting texts.

US carriers only deliver business texts after your number’s 10DLC registration is approved. Until then, texts can be blocked. In the app, say “Lola, run a check” to see exactly where things stand.

Does Lola tell callers she’s an AI and that calls are recorded?

Yes. Every call starts with a short notice that the call may be recorded and that Lola is an AI assistant. See the AI & Call Recording Notice.

I’m a salon’s client and want to book.

Please call or text your salon’s own number — LolaDesk support can’t see salon schedules. Lola on your salon’s line will book you right away.

How do I delete my data or ask a privacy question?

Email legal@loladesk.com. Salon clients: contact the salon, who controls your information. See our Privacy Policy.
