Questions

Good questions.

Does Lola sound natural?

Warm, quick and natural — press “Hear Lola” at the top, or let her call you. She always tells callers she's the salon's AI assistant and that the call may be recorded, as the law requires.

Do I have to change my booking system?

No. Lola works with Square, Vagaro, Boulevard, Fresha, Mindbody, Booksy and Google Calendar — or any system through its calendar link — and never double-books it. Prefer simple? Use LolaDesk's own calendar.

Can I keep my salon's phone number?

Yes. Forward it to Lola when you're busy or after hours — LolaDesk gives you the exact code for your carrier. Clients keep calling the number they know.

What if she can't answer something?

She never guesses. She takes a message and sends it straight to you.

Is it right for a med spa?

Yes — Lola handles consultations, intake questions and bookings. She is not a medical-records system and doesn't store medical details; if your practice needs a Business Associate Agreement, talk to us before you start.

How long does setup take?

About five minutes. Type your salon's name, pick Lola's number, and she learns the rest from your website or menu.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no setup fee. Cancel in Settings and she stays on until the end of your billing period.
